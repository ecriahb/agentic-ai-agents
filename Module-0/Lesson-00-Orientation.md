## 🇬🇧 English Definition

> **Artificial Intelligence is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence.**

Examples: language understanding, classification, prediction, reasoning support, recommendation, image analysis, automation assistance.

## What AI Will Mean in Our DevOps Course

Hum AI ko ek **intelligence layer** ki tarah dekhenge jo existing DevOps systems ke upar baith sakti hai.

```text

                       ↓
```

AI existing DevOps knowledge ko replace nahi karta. Good AI system banane ke liye domain knowledge aur bhi valuable ho jata hai.

## Course Philosophy

### 1. Concepts first
Tool ka command ratne se pehle samjho ki problem kya solve ho rahi hai.

### 2. Python just-in-time
Hume Python developer banna target nahi hai. Jitna AI application build karne ke liye chahiye, utna code properly samjhenge.

### 3. Evidence before conclusion
AI ka confident answer automatically correct answer nahi hota.

### 4. Human approval before risky action
Production delete, Terraform apply, firewall change, secret rotation jaise actions blindly automate nahi karne.

## Common Mistakes

- AI ko magic samajhna.
- ChatGPT use karna = AI engineering samajhna.
- Domain knowledge ko unnecessary samajhna.
- Direct agents/tools par jump karna without model fundamentals.
- Production actions me human verification hata dena.

## 💼 Interview Corner

### Q. What is AI?

**Answer:**
> AI is a field focused on creating systems that perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, making predictions, or assisting decisions.

### Q. How can AI help DevOps teams?

**Answer:**
> AI can assist DevOps teams by analyzing logs, summarizing incidents, reviewing infrastructure changes, correlating operational signals, generating RCA drafts, and recommending remediation while keeping humans in control of high-impact actions.

## 🧠 Remember This

> **Using AI is a skill. Building AI is a career.**

And:

> **AI + DevOps knowledge is more useful than AI without operational context.**
